01 · Introduction

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how MoonArkh ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you download, install, or use our applications on the Google Play Store that link to or reference this policy.

This serves as the general developer-level privacy policy for all Android applications published under the MoonArkh developer account. Because features differ between apps, some apps may collect less data or use fewer third-party tools than described below.

We will never sell your personal data, use it for undisclosed purposes, or share it beyond what is strictly described here. What you read is exactly what happens.
02 · Collection

What We Collect

You provide voluntarily

  • Account credentials: Name, email address, or user ID — only if a specific app offers user accounts or Google Sign-In. Apps without accounts can be used completely anonymously.
  • User-generated content: Text, notes, media, or files you create or process within the App.
  • Support communications: Messages, feedback, and diagnostic logs you email to our team.

Collected automatically

  • Device model, manufacturer, Android OS version, and network status for diagnostics and app optimization.
  • Anonymous crash logs and stack traces (via Google Firebase Crashlytics) to resolve bugs.
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) — only in free, ad-supported apps in compliance with Google Play ad policies.

What we never collect

  • Payment card, debit card, or bank details — Google Play securely processes all transactions
  • Biometric data (fingerprint or facial data)
  • Precise location, camera, or audio — without your explicit on-demand runtime permission
  • Personal data from children under 13, knowingly
03 · Device Permissions

Sensitive Android Permissions

Our Apps may request certain Android runtime permissions to enable specific in-app capabilities. These permissions are requested strictly on-demand, are never accessed without your consent, and can be revoked anytime in your device's System Settings.

Camera
Used only when you take photos, scan documents, or read barcodes/QRs. Never accessed silently in the background.
Microphone & Audio
Used strictly when you record voice, capture audio, or use speech recognition. Never recorded covertly.
Location Services
Used only if an App requires geo-relevant features or local tools. We never track your background location.
Scoped Storage & Photos
Adheres to modern Android Scoped Storage and Photo Picker to let you pick or save files without full device access.
Notifications
On Android 13+, explicit permission is requested to send feature alerts or subscription status notices.
Foreground Services
For ongoing user tasks (playback, recording, downloads) with an active visible notification per Play Console rules.

06 · Subscriptions

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

MoonArkh never sees, handles, or stores your payment card information. All financial transactions are securely managed by Google LLC via Google Play In-App Billing.
  • Billing: Payments are charged to your Google Play account at purchase confirmation.
  • Auto-Renewal: Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing cycle.
  • Renewal Charges: Your account is billed within 24 hours prior to the close of the current period at the confirmed plan rate.
  • Free Trials: Any unused portion of a free trial, if offered, is forfeited upon purchasing a subscription.
  • How to Cancel Anytime: You can manage or cancel your subscription directly in Google Play:
    Open Play Store → Profile icon → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions
    Or online at: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions
  • Refunds: Governed directly by Google Play's standard refund policies at support.google.com/googleplay.
  • Note: Simply uninstalling or deleting the App does not automatically cancel an active subscription.
07 · Deletion

Account & Data Deletion Policy

Google Play Console Policy Mandate

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policy, developers of apps that allow account creation must provide an in-app path and an accessible web resource for users to request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

For apps featuring user accounts, deletion controls are available directly in-app under Settings → Account → Delete Account. When initiated, your account and personal records are permanently deleted within 30 calendar days.

10 · Children

Children's Privacy & Global Compliance

Our Apps are designed for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area / UK). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

If you believe that personal data from a child has been collected inadvertently, please contact hensijada.dev@gmail.com. We will review the matter promptly and delete relevant records immediately.

Any future child-directed apps will be released under separate, specialized configurations that strictly adhere to Google Play's Designed for Families requirements and COPPA regulations.
